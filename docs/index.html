SLOW DOWN. THE TOOLS CAN CATCH UP.

One paper. Three small steps.

  1. 01

    Bring the research.

    Sign in to the full app, upload a PDF or paste an arXiv ID, and choose your level.

  2. 02

    Let it prepare.

    Background jobs build your learning blocks. Check progress in your private library.

  3. 03

    Read. Learn. Wander.

    Explore, study or listen. Export your edition so the work is yours to keep.

ⓘ The full app runs on free Render hosting. It can sleep or lose temporary data after a restart; export editions you want to keep. Overnight readiness is not guaranteed.
